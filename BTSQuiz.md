## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Personal Information You Provide
When you create an account or log in to the App using **Google Sign-In**, we may collect the following information provided by Google Authentication:
* **Account Credentials:** Name, email address, and profile picture URL.
* **Unique User ID:** Firebase User ID (`uid`) generated upon authentication.

### B. App Data and Progress
To save your game state and provide leaderboard or quiz tracking features, we store:
* Quiz scores, completed levels, achievements, and user preferences.
* Timestamp of quiz activities.

### C. Automatically Collected Information & Device Data
When you access the App, third-party service providers (such as Google AdMob and Firebase) may automatically collect certain device information, including:
* Device hardware model, operating system version, and unique device identifiers (e.g., Advertising ID).
* IP address, language settings, and general location data (country/city level).
* App performance data, crash logs, and diagnostics.
